Certified Color Analysis, Explained

What is color analysis, really?

A friendly, no-jargon breakdown of the method, the seasons, and what to expect at your appointment.

The Simple Version

It's science, not guesswork.

Every person has an undertone — a subtle warmth or coolness beneath the skin — along with a natural level of contrast between skin, hair, and eyes. Personal color analysis identifies those two things, then matches them to a family of colors that enhances your natural coloring rather than competing with it.

The result is a personalized palette: dozens of specific, wearable shades that make your skin look clear and healthy, your eyes brighter, and your whole look more polished — often without changing anything else.

The Draping Method

During your session, we hold a sequence of fabric drapes up to your face, under neutral lighting, and observe how each one affects your complexion in real time. Some colors will visibly lift and brighten you; others will flatten or wash you out. It's a genuinely fun, "aha"-filled process — most clients are amazed at how obvious the difference becomes once they see it.

The Four Palettes

Spring, Summer, Autumn & Winter

Every client falls into one of four seasonal color families, each with its own undertone, depth, and saturation.

Spring

Warm, clear, and bright. Think golden peach, coral, and warm grass green.

Summer

Cool, soft, and muted. Think dusty rose, soft lavender, and powder blue.

Autumn

Warm, rich, and earthy. Think rust, olive, mustard, and deep camel.

Winter

Cool, deep, and high-contrast. Think true red, icy blue, and emerald.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a color analysis session take?

Most sessions run 60–90 minutes depending on the package you choose. The Signature Analysis includes extra time for a wardrobe conversation and Q&A.

Can color analysis be done virtually?

Yes! Virtual sessions use the same draping method over video, with guidance on lighting and camera setup beforehand to ensure accurate results.

What should I wear or bring to my appointment?

Come with minimal makeup and, if possible, your natural hair color visible. Feel free to bring a few current wardrobe favorites if you'd like feedback on what to keep or donate.

What if I've had my colors done before?

Skin tone, hair color, and even personal style preferences can shift over the years. A refresh session is a great, lower-cost way to confirm or update your palette.
