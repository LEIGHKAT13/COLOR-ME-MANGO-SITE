Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026. Here is how Color Me Mango collects, uses, and protects your information, especially your photos.

Information I Collect

When you book, contact me, or use this website, I may collect your name, email address, phone number, questionnaire answers, any notes you share, and the photos you send for your color analysis. If you buy a gift, I also collect the recipient's name and email so I can send them their welcome email.

How I Use Your Information

I use your information only to complete your color analysis, send your results, respond to your questions, and handle payments. I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

Your Photos

Your photos are used only for your color analysis. I delete your photos within 30 days after your results are delivered. Your finished results and palette guide may be kept so I can help you with questions later.

I will never share your photos publicly without your written permission. If I would love to feature your photos or results (for example, on Instagram or TikTok), I will ask you first, and you are always free to say no.

Children

Children ages 7 and up can receive a color analysis, but a parent or guardian must book, complete the questionnaire, and send the photos. Photos of children are never shared publicly without the parent or guardian's written permission.

Payments

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never stored by Color Me Mango.

Services That Help Run This Website

A few trusted services help run this website: Stripe (payments), Netlify (website hosting), Web3Forms (delivering contact and booking form messages to my email), and Google Workspace (business email). They only receive the information needed to do their part.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Stripe may use cookies on its checkout page to keep payments secure.

Your Choices

You can ask me to see, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by emailing leigh@colormemango.com.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, I will update it on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email me at leigh@colormemango.com.
