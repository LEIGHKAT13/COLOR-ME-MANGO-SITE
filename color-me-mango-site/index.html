The Basics

What exactly is color analysis?

Personal color analysis is the art and science of identifying which colors work in harmony with your unique skin tone, eye color, and hair color. I study photos you take at home, using my simple photo guide, to see how different colors work with your natural coloring. From there, I uncover the palette that makes you look naturally healthy, vibrant, and put-together.

Every person falls into one of twelve seasons, nested within the four classic seasons of Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter, each with its own undertone and range of shades. Once you know yours, choosing what to wear (and what to buy) becomes effortless.
