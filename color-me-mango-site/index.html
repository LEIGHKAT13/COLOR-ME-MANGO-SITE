The Basics

What exactly is color analysis?

Personal color analysis is the art and science of identifying which colors work in harmony with your unique skin tone, eye color, and hair color. Using a method called draping — holding different fabric swatches near your face — we uncover the palette that makes you look naturally healthy, vibrant, and put-together.

Every person falls into one of four seasonal color families: Spring, Summer, Autumn, or Winter, each with its own undertone and range of shades. Once you know yours, choosing what to wear (and what to buy) becomes effortless.
