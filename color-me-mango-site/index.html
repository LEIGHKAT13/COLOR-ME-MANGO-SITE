The Basics

What exactly is color analysis?

It's finding the colors that work in harmony with your natural skin, hair, and eyes. Here's how it works with me:

  • I study photos you take at home, using my simple photo guide
  • I look at your undertone, chroma, and contrast
  • You're matched to one of 12 seasons within Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter
  • You get a palette that makes getting dressed (and shopping) easy

Why a Real Analyst

Real eyes. Real training. Not an algorithm.

AI apps can guess at a season from a photo. Your analysis is done by me, a certified color analyst, looking at you.

AI color apps

  • Read the whole photo, lighting and background included
  • Can't reliably see undertone or chroma
  • Often land people in seasons that don't fit them
  • Give a label with no explanation

Your analysis with me

  • I study your skin, hair, and eyes, not the room around you
  • Trained to read undertone, chroma, and contrast
  • I compare neighboring seasons before deciding
  • I explain why you landed in your season
  • If your lighting is off, I'll ask for a retake instead of guessing
