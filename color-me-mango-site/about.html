The Face Behind Color Me Mango

Hi, I'm Leigh.

✓ Certified Color Analyst ✓ 500+ Sessions Delivered

I help people fall back in love with getting dressed — one honest, joyful color analysis session at a time.

"Wear the colors that let people see you first."

I have ALWAYS loved color—especially how certain shades bring different emotions and how the right colors can completely change the way you look AND feel. Discovering color analysis took that love to another level. Once I knew my own palette, getting dressed became so much easier. I could open my closet, throw pieces together, and know they would work together and make my complexion glow. That’s what made me want to help other people experience the same thing.

Why I Do This

Confidence shouldn't be a guessing game.

[Editable placeholder]: I became passionate about color analysis because I watched too many talented, wonderful people quietly believe that "nothing looks good on them." It's almost never true — it's usually just the wrong colors. Once someone sees their true palette held up against their face for the first time, something shifts. Shoulders relax. Eyes light up. That reaction is why I do this work, session after session.

My approach is warm, unhurried, and judgment-free. Whether you're preparing for a wardrobe refresh, a big life event, or you simply want to stop second-guessing every purchase, I'll meet you exactly where you are.

My Promise to You

  • A relaxed, welcoming session — no pressure, no judgment.
  • Honest, personalized guidance grounded in certified training.
  • A take-home palette you'll actually use for years.
  • Practical shopping and styling tips tailored to your life.
