The Service

Color Me Mango offers virtual personal color analysis. Your analysis is done from the questionnaire and photos you send, using my photo guide. With the Peach package, you also get a live Zoom call to go over your results.

Booking & Payment

Payment is due in full when you book. Payments are processed securely by Stripe. All prices are in U.S. dollars.

Timeframes

Your results are delivered within 5 business days of when I receive your photos. Your timeframe starts once your photos are received, not when you book.

The 24-Hour Rush add-on (available with the Mango package) means your results are delivered within 24 hours of when I receive your photos, weekends included.

If your photos don't follow the photo guide closely enough for an accurate analysis, I will ask you to retake them, and your timeframe starts once the new photos arrive.

Refunds & Cancellations

You can cancel for a full refund any time before you send your photos. Once your photos are submitted, your analysis has begun and refunds are no longer available.

Unsent Photos

If I haven't received your photos, I will send you a reminder after 1 week. If your photos still haven't arrived 15 days after your purchase, your booking expires and will not be refunded.
